I am an Applied Scientist at Amazon, working on causal inference and machine learning for marketing measurement. I received my Ph.D. in Statistics from Boston University in 2025.

My research interests span time-series modeling, statistical learning, and foundation models. My doctoral research focused on nonparametric methods for stochastic volatility models with heavy-tailed errors. More recently, I have been exploring how statistical structure can inform foundation models for time-series forecasting, with an emphasis on uncertainty and robustness under distribution shift.
